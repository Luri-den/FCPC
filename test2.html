Before You Explore

This map documents historic pioneer cemeteries and burial grounds in Franklin County, Indiana. Please read the following before visiting a site or using this information.

Access and Private Property

A cemetery's appearance on this map does not give anyone the right to enter it. Many of these sites are on private land, and Indiana landowners are generally not required to allow public access to cemeteries on private property. Always obtain the landowner's permission before entering.

Protecting These Sites

Do not disturb, move, remove, or damage any marker, stone, or other feature. Indiana law protects cemeteries and burial grounds, and certain construction that disturbs the ground within 100 feet of a cemetery requires review by the Indiana DNR Division of Historic Preservation and Archaeology (DHPA).

If you find human remains, or think you may have, leave them undisturbed and report them promptly to the County Coroner, the DNR Division of Law Enforcement, and the DHPA.

About This Information

This is a work in progress. Records come from ongoing surveys and may be incomplete or contain errors. Locations are approximate unless stated otherwise and must not be used for legal, boundary, or property purposes. Contact and correction information will be added when the site is published.
