Before You Explore

Welcome to the Franklin County Pioneer Cemetery Survey map. Pioneer cemeteries are sensitive historical sites. Many exist on private property or working farmland.

  • Always respect property boundaries and obtain landowner permission where required.
  • GPS markers and grave plots are intended for genealogical reference, not exact turn-by-turn navigation.
  • Do not disturb markers or grounds.
